Cyril Power

Biography Artworks

British, 1872–1951

A prolific and pioneering maker of color linocuts, Cyril Power is known for images that captured the hustle and bustle of an industrializing world. Many of his works, depicting cars, sporting events, and other dynamic scenes, were produced in collaboration with his creative partner Sybil Andrews; the two are both noted alumni of the Grosvenor School of Modern Art. Among their projects are posters designed for the London Passengers Transport Board between 1929 and 1937. Power’s artworks rarely appear at auctions but are leading lots when they surface; a print of his famous image Speed Trial, inspired by the record-breaking race car Bluebird, fetched $128,386 at a 2012 sale. Power’s images convey the efficiency of modern technology and life, with hard-edged but sinuous lines that convey a strong sense of movement. Toward the end of his life, he also created oil paintings. Power’s works are in collections including the British Museum, the Museum of Modern Art, and the National Gallery of Australia.
