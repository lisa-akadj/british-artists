In my opinion, early to mid 20th century English art is one of the most underrated part of art history.

While I worked at a fine art logistics company I had experiences that exposed me to arts of different eras that I had no knowledge of. I realised that the artworks that would catch my eye were British moden art. I noticed that artworks from that era are nowhere near as popular as 'Mona Lisa' or 'The Starry Night'.

Perhaps early to mid 20th century British artworks are not very popular because that part of history is tainted by two world wars and still is painful to remember. But also there have been many British artists from the 20th century such as Francis Bacon, Henry Moore and Barbara Hepworth, whose reputations eclipse those of the artist I would like to talk about today.
